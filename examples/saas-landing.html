Questions

Do I need to change my tracking?

No. Fernhill reads the events you already send through Segment, RudderStack or our HTTP API.

How is this different from a dashboard tool?

Dashboards show numbers and leave the reading to you. Fernhill reads them for you and writes down what changed and why.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU (Frankfurt) or the US (Virginia), whichever you choose. Raw events are deleted after 90 days.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans stop at the end of the period, and you can export every report as Markdown.
